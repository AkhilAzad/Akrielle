# 1. Product Overview

Akrielle is an AI-powered Beauty Intelligence Platform.

The platform helps users discover beauty recommendations personalized to their own facial characteristics instead of generic beauty trends.

The first version focuses on delivering a professional Beauty Profile after analyzing a selfie.

---

# 2. Product Goal

Within 30 seconds of visiting Akrielle, a user should be able to:

1. Upload a selfie.
2. Receive AI facial analysis.
3. Understand their Beauty Profile.
4. Receive personalized makeup recommendations.
5. Understand why those recommendations were made.

# 11. Recommendation Philosophy

Akrielle never tells users whether they are attractive.

Instead it recommends styles that complement their natural features.

Example

Incorrect

"You are 8/10."

Correct

"Warm nude lipsticks complement your warm undertone and enhance your natural complexion."

# 22. Development Principles

Every feature must answer:

Does this solve a real user problem?

Is it better than existing solutions?

Would users recommend Akrielle because of it?

If the answer is "No," the feature should not be built.
